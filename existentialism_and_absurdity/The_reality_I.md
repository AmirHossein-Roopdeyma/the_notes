# The reality

[← Back to Existentialism and Absurdity](./)

The reality often hurts. It is indeed a slap in the face when fantasies fade away and the true realization of the world begins to exist. One can always live in delusion and never embrace the truth, but will they ever experience the contentment humankind deserves to enjoy?

If  regret and resentment are inevitable parts of life, why do we sacrifice the the sweat thoughts in out mind for the harsh moments of realization? Whether it is better to know nothing and live a fabricated life or to accept the possibility of loss in pursuit of our desires is a dilemma we tend to confront.

Nevertheless, every move, despite being calculated and harmonized with nature rules, could lead to a failure. Thus, one may ask: should I remain constantly sad yet fine, or be temporality devastated and yet one step closer to the truth?

Wisdom often prohibits actions while courage demands it. Yet when wisdom and courage are in a battlefield against each other, the decision is often handed over to emotions, biased and reluctant to lead us toward a better resolution.
