# The Relief 

[← Back to Existentialism and Absurdity](./)

I had a flight a few weeks ago. Flight is always associated with a plethora of uncertainties for me. This has been the case almost always. I think about the things I love before takeoff and cherish the good moments with people I love the most. This time I was entirely detached from the reality. Nothing mattered to me as if I was ready to surrender whatever illusion of control I had. I never used to listen to music during a takeoff and every air bump used to be like a nightmare. But this time it was different, I put on the headphones and listened to my favorite rock music. From the third person point of view I was a man sitting there, smiling. I simply didn't care. I experienced a new sensation and a sense of freedom I never had, a super-independent feeling induced within helpless creature sitting there and smiling. That fed my avoidant tendencies and transformed them from a flaw into a relief. I couldn't care less whether I would step off the plane at the end of the journey. Things are changing rapidly in my mind. So rapidly that I can no longer keep the track of the modifications. I want to embrace this feeling and nurture it; a sense of power in the most powerless position, a curiosity despite the odds and the voluntary acceptance of surrender without remorse.
