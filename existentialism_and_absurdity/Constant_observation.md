# Constant Observation

[← Back to Existentialism and Absurdity](./)

Who are we to cast the suspicious, judgmental looks upon the people we interact with? Not all of us are caring enough to understand the burden of responsibility we may have toward others. Yet if i refuse to judge and draw conclusions of the behaviors, I become a certain victim of social life.
The identification of Defection and the decision to take action often come together and yet sometimes satisfaction of detection overweight the value of enforcement. This may satisfy the inner voice that says, "ok others' feelings matter and at leas you have been practising cautious". The caveat is that one may forget gradually their responsibility toward themselves.
Whether to enforce and counteract or absorb the pain of being deemed as a naive, becomes the question. This so-called naivety is one of the smartest thing a human being can perform; nevertheless, the intellectual integrity of such performer is often questioned. At what point does compassion cease to be a virtue and become a betrayal of oneself?
