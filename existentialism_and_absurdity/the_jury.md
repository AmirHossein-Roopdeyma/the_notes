# The jury

[← Back to Existentialism and Absurdity](./)

Who is the jury? who is the judge? I ask myself.
We are in the trial of your own morality. I respond.
How do you plead? guilty or not guilty?
I hesitate to answer, the judge asks again this time with a stronger voice as if he has already decided the verdict. 
I don't know the answer but I have to make up something based on the facts and the backups I can provide. The mind flies and wanders around, evaluating so many uncertainties and ifs. 
What is wrong and what is right? I have a few seconds to respond. The jury is observing and every moment could make a terrible impression. 
Your Honour, I need more time. I know I am not guilty, yet I fear I will fail to prove it once the consequences are weighed
The trial falls into absolute silence, nobody knows what is happening. At this moment I remember the cause and the very reason why I should not declare myself guilty. 
I remember the moment I decided to act, to escape the anxiety and despair. 
Someone speaks around the clock whispering in my ear about surrender about letting everything go. So many people are watching, this makes me nervous and frustrated. 
Yet no one has a clue of what is correct or not. The world is just like the way it was yesterday. 
Every night I go to this court and defend myself but my defense is not convincing. So tomorrow I will try it again.
